## Our Core Pillars & Capabilities

### 1. Strategic Land Identification & Master Planning
We acquire land parcels located strictly in high-appreciation growth belts—connected to major arterial corridors of bengaluru. Every layout is master-planned with wide internal avenues, efficient plot demarcations, rainwater harvesting, and landscaped community parks.

### 2. Comprehensive Statutory Approvals & Legal Governance
Land safety is non-negotiable. Across our growing portfolio of developments:
- **Jurisdictional Planning Sanctions:** Every layout is sanctioned by the relevant planning authority for that specific micro-market—including **BMRDA, BDA, DTCP, MPA, BIAAPA**, or other designated statutory development bodies.
- **RERA Compliance:** Layouts are registered with RERA wherever applicable, ensuring stringent consumer protection and timely delivery.
- **Clear Title Dossiers:** Complete documentation kits (mother deeds, conversion orders, layout sanctions, ECs, and legal opinions) are made available to prospective buyers upfront.

### 3. Execution-First Development
We do not sell raw earth on speculative promises. Our layouts feature:
- Asphalted / concrete internal roads with pedestrian curbs
- Underground electricity conduits and energy-efficient LED street lighting
- Underground Drainage (UGD) and dedicated storm water drain networks
- 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance and secure compound boundary walls
- Children’s play parks, tree-lined boulevards, and landscaped open zones

### 4. End-to-End Buyer Support & Financial Enablement
We partner with leading public and private sector banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Bank of Baroda, etc.) to offer hassle-free home/plot loan approvals with minimal paperwork. Our dedicated customer success team assists through every step:
- Private, guided site visits
- Legal documentation review & verification
- Bank loan facilitation & coordination
- Stamp duty, registration, and khata documentation assistance

## Standardized Brand Guidelines & Copywriting Notes

For all marketing, digital communications, brochures, and web copy, adhere to the following conventions:

1. **Attributing Experience & Track Record:**
   - **Do NOT claim:** *"Aloka Developers Private Limited was founded 10 years ago and has delivered 25 projects."* (Incorrect — company was incorporated in 2026).
   - **DO state:** *"Aloka Developers is backed by a leadership team with 10+ years of domain expertise, 25+ successfully delivered plotted projects, and over 1,500 happy customer relationships across Bengaluru."*

2. **Authority Approvals & Project Generalization:**
   - **Do NOT restrict the company identity to only one approval body** (e.g., do not say *"Aloka Developers only sells MPA-approved plots"*).
   - **DO state:** *"All Aloka projects carry 100% legal clearance and layout approvals from the respective competent planning authorities (such as BMRDA, BDA, DTCP, MPA, BIAAPA) and RERA compliance."*
   - For specific project collateral (e.g., Aloka Green County in Malur), specify the exact project sanction (e.g., *MPA Approved under BMRDA*). For corporate collateral, use generalized authority approval language.
