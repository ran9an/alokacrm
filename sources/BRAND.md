## Audience

- **Primary**: Families looking to own residential land in Bengaluru and build toward their future home.
- **Secondary**: Long-term residential land buyers seeking steady appreciation in growth corridors.
- **Expertise**: Intermediate to beginner (first-time and family land buyers)
- **Active problems**:
  - Fear of unclear documentation, illegal layouts, and approval ambiguity (Khata issues, DC conversion, Encumbrance).
  - Uncertainty over whether promised infrastructure (roads, water, electricity, drainage) will actually be delivered.
  - Frustration with aggressive sales tactics, artificial urgency, and exaggerated appreciation claims.
  - Lack of post-sale and registration support from traditional developers.
- **Common misconceptions**:
  - Plotted layouts in peripheral growth corridors inherently have legal grey areas or approval risks.
  - Buyers must pay an exorbitant brand premium with mega-developers to secure clear titles and quality infrastructure.
  - High capital appreciation requires taking speculative risks on unapproved agricultural land.

## Editorial Rules

### Always do
- Lead with verifiable facts, documentation proof, and confirmed regulatory approvals.
- Use grounded realities, actual infrastructure status, and transparent timelines.
- Educate buyers with clear, actionable due diligence steps for land acquisition and registration.
- Maintain an active, human, direct, and professional tone.
- Make numbers, names, dates, and mechanisms specific rather than abstract.

### Never do
- Never promise guaranteed returns, doubling investments, or speculative appreciation rates.
- Never use artificial urgency ("Hurry before it's too late!", "Once-in-a-lifetime opportunity").
- Never use generic AI-slop vocabulary, throat-clearing openers, or empty corporate buzzwords.
- Never rely on unverified claims, ungrounded future speculation, or negative attacks on competitors.
