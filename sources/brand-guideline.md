# Aloka Developers --- Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0\
**Brand:** Aloka Developers Private Limited\
**Primary Application:** Social media, digital marketing, project
marketing, brochures, presentations, and AI-generated creative direction

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Brand Overview

Aloka Developers Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based plot development
company focused on delivering legally clear, fully developed residential
plots across East Bengaluru's growth corridors.

The brand should communicate:

-   Trust
-   Legal clarity
-   Quality development
-   Strategic location
-   Long-term ownership value
-   Premium but approachable real estate

Aloka should look like a **professional real estate developer**, not a
typical property broker.

### Brand Essence

**Trusted land. Developed properly. Located for tomorrow.**

### Brand Personality

  Attribute           Brand Expression
  ------------------- -----------------------------------------------
  Trustworthy         Clear, factual, transparent
  Premium             Spacious, refined, polished
  Modern              Contemporary typography and layouts
  Grounded            Real project photography and proof
  Confident           Strong messaging without hype
  Approachable        Human, simple, easy to understand
  Investment-minded   Location, infrastructure, and long-term value
  Family-oriented     Land that can become a future home

# 4. Typography System

Typography is a major part of Aloka's visual identity.

The brand uses three complementary typefaces:

1.  **Anton** --- Primary Display
2.  **Inter** --- Secondary / Body
3.  **Libre Baskerville** --- Accent

**Manrope** may be used as an approved alternative to Inter where
required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.1 Primary Display --- Anton

**Font:** Anton

**Role:** Primary display typeface

Use Anton for:

-   Major campaign headlines
-   Project names
-   Large numbers
-   Location claims
-   Key USPs
-   Hero statements
-   Social media cover headlines
-   High-impact advertising copy

### Typography Treatment

Aloka's Anton treatment should generally use:

-   **ALL CAPS**
-   Slightly **negative letter spacing**
-   Strong visual scale
-   Short phrases
-   High contrast
-   Large display sizes

### Examples

**ALOKA**

**GREEN COUNTY**

**3 KM**

**FROM STRR**

**PREMIUM PLOTS**

**BUILD WHERE BENGALURU IS GROWING**

### Letter Spacing

Use slightly negative tracking when appropriate.

Recommended starting point:

**Tracking: approximately -1% to -3%**

Adjust optically based on size and word length.

The objective is a compact, bold, editorial display rather than loose
typography.

### Important Rule

Do not use Anton for long paragraphs.

Anton is a **display font**, not a body font.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Secondary Font --- Inter

**Font:** Inter

**Role:** Primary supporting and body typeface

Use Inter for:

-   Supporting headlines
-   Body copy
-   Location details
-   Project information
-   Amenities
-   Specifications
-   CTA copy
-   Captions
-   Website and digital UI
-   Informational graphics

### Typography Treatment

Use:

-   Sentence case for normal information
-   Uppercase for short labels
-   Strong weight contrast
-   Slightly negative letter spacing where visually appropriate

### Recommended Weight Hierarchy

  Use                         Weight
  --------------------------- ----------
  Large supporting headline   700--800
  Section heading             600--700
  Subheading                  500--600
  Body copy                   400--500
  Small information           400--500

### Letter Spacing

Inter may use slightly negative tracking, particularly for:

-   Large headlines
-   Short labels
-   Navigation
-   CTA text

Recommended starting point:

**Tracking: approximately -1% to -2%**

Optical adjustment takes priority over fixed numerical values.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Approved Alternative --- Manrope

**Font:** Manrope

Manrope is an approved alternative to Inter when a more contemporary
geometric appearance is desired.

Use Manrope primarily for:

-   Digital campaigns
-   Website interfaces
-   Modern social media layouts
-   UI-style information cards
-   Supporting typography

### Important Rule

Do not mix **Inter and Manrope randomly within the same creative**.

Choose one secondary sans-serif system for a particular campaign or
design system.

### Default

**Inter remains the primary secondary font.**

**Manrope is the approved alternative.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Accent Font --- Libre Baskerville

**Font:** Libre Baskerville

**Role:** Premium editorial accent

Libre Baskerville should be used selectively.

It adds:

-   Elegance
-   Heritage
-   Sophistication
-   Emotional warmth
-   Premium editorial character

### Best Uses

Use Libre Baskerville for:

-   Short emotional statements
-   Premium taglines
-   Supporting campaign phrases
-   Editorial-style subheads
-   Lifestyle messaging
-   Selective emphasis

### Examples

*Buy Your Dream Plot*

*Your next chapter begins here.*

*Land for the life you imagine.*

### Usage Rule

Libre Baskerville should remain an **accent**, not the dominant
typography.

Recommended visual ratio:

**Anton: 50--60%**\
**Inter / Manrope: 30--40%**\
**Libre Baskerville: 10--20%**

The exact ratio can change depending on the creative.

# 9. Typography Rules

### Do

-   Use Anton for high-impact headlines.
-   Use uppercase Anton for major display messaging.
-   Use slightly negative tracking for large display typography.
-   Use Inter for clarity and information.
-   Use Libre Baskerville sparingly.
-   Maintain strong contrast between headline and supporting copy.
-   Keep headlines short.
-   Use typography as a visual element, not just information.

### Don't

-   Use Anton for paragraphs.
-   Use Libre Baskerville for large blocks of information.
-   Mix multiple serif fonts.
-   Use more than three typefaces in one creative.
-   Use decorative fonts.
-   Use excessive letter spacing.
-   Use inconsistent font styles between project creatives.
-   Change typography treatment randomly from post to post.

imagine themselves physically standing at the property.

# 16. Logo Usage

The Aloka logo should always retain adequate breathing room.

### Primary Logo

Use the approved full-color Aloka Developers Private Limited logo on
light backgrounds.

### Dark Backgrounds

Use the approved light/white version where available.

### Do Not

-   Stretch the logo
-   Rotate the logo
-   Change logo colors
-   Add unnecessary shadows
-   Put the logo over busy imagery
-   Alter the proportions
-   Recreate the logo using substitute typography

### Clear Space

Maintain a protected area around the logo approximately equal to the
height of the primary logo mark.

Standardize CTAs across marketing creatives.

### Primary CTA

**BOOK A SITE VISIT**

### Secondary CTA

**CALL / WHATSAPP**

-   BUY NOW!!!
-   HURRY!!!

# 27. The Aloka Brand Standard

Every piece of communication should pass five tests:

### 1. Is it trustworthy?

Would a buyer believe this?

### 2. Is it clear?

Can the message be understood quickly?

### 3. Is it premium?

Does it look like a professional developer?

### 4. Is it factual?

Can every important claim be verified?

### 5. Is it recognizably Aloka?

Would someone identify the brand without needing to see the logo?

If the answer is yes to all five, the creative is on-brand.
